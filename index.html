What We Offer

Turn Your Property Into Immediate Tax Savings and Long-Term Wealth

Engineering-Based Cost Segregation Studies

We deliver IRS-compliant, engineering-based cost segregation studies that identify and reclassify property components into shorter depreciation lives (5, 7, and 15 years).


Our approach combines:

  • Detailed asset-level analysis using construction and cost data
  • Industry-standard methodologies aligned with IRS Audit Techniques Guide
  • Optional site inspections for higher-value properties

Outcome: Accelerated depreciation, increased first-year deductions, and immediate cash flow improvement.

Accelerated Depreciation & Tax Strategy Optimization

We go beyond reports—we help you maximize tax savings through strategic depreciation planning.


Our team evaluates:

  • Eligibility for 100% bonus depreciation (post-2025 rules)
  • Optimal timing for studies (purchase, renovation, or retroactive)
  • Integration with your broader tax strategy (REP status, passive vs active income)

Outcome: Optimized tax positions, reduced taxable income, and improved ROI across your portfolio.

Investor-Focused Analysis & Audit-Ready Deliverables

We provide clear, investor-friendly insights along with fully documented, audit-ready reports.


You receive:

  • Detailed asset schedules categorized by depreciation life
  • Comprehensive engineering report supporting IRS compliance
  • ROI and cash flow impact summaries for informed decision-making

Outcome: Confidence in audits, clarity in numbers, and actionable insights to scale your real estate investments.
